# PROBLEM 4: INACCESSIBLE SRHR SERVICES FOR ADOLESCENT GIRLS AND YOUNG WOMEN
## The Problem:
Adolescent girls and young women in sub-Saharan Africa face a convergence of barriers when attempting to access sexual and reproductive health and rights (SRHR) services, including contraception, STI testing, menstrual health support, information on family planning and antenatal care.
## These barriers are:
Structural: distance, cost, missing commodities
Cultural: stigma, parental consent requirements, negative provider attitudes
Informational: lack of awareness of available services and rights
The consequences include high rates of unintended pregnancy, HIV infection, and preventable maternal morbidity.
Young women and girls aged 10 to 24 lack a discreet, age-appropriate, and locally relevant digital platform through which they can access accurate SRHR information, locate youth-friendly health facilities near them, ask anonymous questions, and understand their rights, without fear of stigma, disclosure, or community judgment.
## Who Is Affected:
Adolescent girls and young women aged 10-24.
## Where Technology Could Help:
A mobile-first, privacy-centered SRHR information and service-finder platform with anonymous Q&A, a verified youth-friendly facility locator, and age-appropriate content in local languages.
